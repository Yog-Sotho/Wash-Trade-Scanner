## Calibration

Rates shipped in `config/detector_calibration.json`, measured on the development seeds. A hit's score is P(wash | hit) by Bayes' rule with prior WASH_PRIOR = 0.01 and the 95% upper bound of the clean-pool false-positive rate; a trade is labelled wash at ≥ 0.8 (SUSPICIOUS_ACTIVITY_THRESHOLD). ## Isolation Forest (leave-one-pool-out)

The reference model (default features and contamination) is fitted on the other clean pools, its threshold set there to a 1% false-positive rate (ML_TARGET_FPR), and measured on the held-out pool's labelled scenarios. PR-AUC of a random ranking equals the prevalence.

| Held-out pool | PR-AUC | Prevalence (random PR-AUC) | Recall | Clean FPR |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `univ2_usdc_weth` | 0.006 | 0.006 | 0.000 | 2.92% |
| `univ3_usdc_weth_005` | 0.001 | 0.001 | 0.000 | 0.06% |
| `univ3_weth_usdt_005` | 0.001 | 0.001 | 0.000 | 0.28% |
| **mean** | 0.002 ± 0.002 | | 0.000 | 1.09% |

`wash-train` measures every model it trains the same way, stores the rates in the model file (audits score the model's hits with them), and refuses to save a model whose PR-AUC is under 2.0× that of a random ranking (ML_MIN_PR_AUC_LIFT).

## Documented wash-trading market

`nexf_weth`: NexF/WETH Uniswap V2: the market of the FBI NexFundAI sting token (Operation Token Mirrors).

This pool is linked on chain to the FBI's NexFundAI sting (Operation Token Mirrors; charges announced October 2024, against market makers who wash-traded the token on Uniswap). Kaiko's analysis of the NexFundAI market identified the funding address `0x4aa6a6231630ad13ef52c06de3d3d3850fafcd70`; that address sent WETH 15 times (blocks 20,587,745-20,778,730) to contract `0x8a5d8700dd19488d964d202e87b0cc85704054db`, which swapped in this pool 58 times (blocks 20,598,086-20,727,020), and the pool traded from May to September 2024, the sting's timeline. Those 58 swaps are all sells of NexF, in pairs from two operator accounts a few blocks apart: inventory distribution, not a wash pattern, so they are not used as positive labels. Kaiko describes the wash trading itself as multi-wallet (148 of 485 wallets first funded in the same block as at least five others), which only funding-link data can expose; the fixture records swaps, not funding transfers.

Phase 2's detectors label none of it: the documented mechanism is multi-wallet, and funding links are not in the recorded data. This is the largest open gap (see docs/project/roadmap_to_9.md).
